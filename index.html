About Us

辽宁陆海星通信科技有限公司

Liaoning Luhaixing Communication Technology Co., Ltd.

辽宁陆海星通信科技有限公司（简称陆海星），是工业软件产品开发商及解决方案提供商。陆海星聚焦中国工业4.0、智慧市政等领域的智能管控产品开发及行业解决方案，丰富的产品线融入到工业互联网体系的各个环节，提供从自动化、数字化到智能化的多层次的软硬件产品及行业解决方案。

Liaoning Luhaixing Communication Technology Co., Ltd. (referred to as Luhaixing) is an industrial software product developer and solution provider. Luhaixing focuses on the development of intelligent control products and industry solutions in China's Industry 4.0, smart municipal and other fields. Its rich product line integrates into all links of the industrial Internet system, providing multi-level software and hardware products and industry solutions from automation, digitalization to intelligence.

我们愿用自主技术、产品、服务形成的中国的产业生态，成为细分领域的"隐形冠军"来服务于中国制造，助力中国制造业实现数字化转型与智能化升级。

We are willing to use the Chinese industrial ecosystem formed by independent technology, products and services to become a "hidden champion" in the segmented field to serve Chinese manufacturing and help Chinese manufacturing achieve digital transformation and intelligent upgrading.
